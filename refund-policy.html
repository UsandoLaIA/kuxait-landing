Refund Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Kuxait LLC

This Refund Policy describes when and how Kuxait LLC ("Kuxait", "we", "us") issues refunds for its services, and how cancellations and rescheduling work. It forms part of our Terms & Conditions. Where an accepted Proposal states different cancellation or refund conditions, the Proposal prevails for that engagement.

1. Scope

This policy applies to all services sold by Kuxait: implementation engagements structured in stages, ongoing monthly engagements, team training, leadership sessions and, where offered, digital products such as templates or recorded content.

2. Principles

  • Our services are professional services. Once work on a stage or session has started, the time and expertise invested cannot be recovered, so refunds are tied to work not yet performed.
  • Every engagement reserves time in our calendar. Deposits exist to protect that reservation.
  • When we fail to deliver what was scoped, we fix it or we refund the part we did not deliver. See section 7.
  • We respond to every refund request in writing within 10 business days.

3. Implementation engagements (fixed-price stages)

Implementation engagements covering stages 1 to 5 of our method are quoted at a fixed price and begin with an initial payment that reserves the start date (the "Deposit"). Unless the Proposal states otherwise, the Deposit is 20% of the fixed price.

When you cancelWhat is refunded
14 or more calendar days before the agreed kickoff dateAll amounts paid, except the Deposit, which is retained.
Fewer than 14 calendar days before kickoffAll amounts paid beyond the Deposit and beyond any stage already started. The Deposit is retained.
After kickoffFees for stages not yet started are refunded in full if prepaid. Fees for stages completed or in progress are not refundable.

A stage is considered "in progress" from the moment Kuxait has conducted the first interview, workshop, mapping session or configuration work belonging to that stage. Deliverables already provided remain the Client's property subject to payment of the corresponding stage.

If the Client pauses an engagement for more than 60 consecutive days without an agreed plan to resume, Kuxait may close the engagement and apply the "after kickoff" rule above.

4. Monthly engagements (stages 6 and 7 and other retainers)

  • Monthly engagements are invoiced in advance and renew automatically each month.
  • Either party may cancel with 30 days' written notice. The month in course is not prorated and is not refunded.
  • Any months prepaid beyond the notice period are refunded in full.
  • Unused sessions within a month do not roll over unless agreed in writing.

5. Training and leadership sessions

Notice before the sessionOptions
7 or more calendar daysFull refund, or reschedule at no cost.
Between 48 hours and 7 daysReschedule once at no cost, or refund of 50% of the session fee.

For session packages, these rules apply to each individual session. Unused sessions in a package can be refunded in full if the cancellation of the whole package is notified at least 7 days before the next scheduled session.

Non-refundable travel and accommodation costs already incurred for on-site sessions are charged to the Client regardless of the notice period, with supporting receipts.

6. Digital products

Where Kuxait sells digital products such as templates, toolkits or recorded training, you may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase provided the product has not been substantially accessed or downloaded. Once a download has occurred or more than 20% of a recorded program has been viewed, the purchase is final, except where required by applicable consumer law.

7. If Kuxait cancels or fails to deliver

  • If Kuxait cancels a session, you may choose a full refund of that session or a rescheduled date.
  • If Kuxait is unable to start an engagement on the agreed date for reasons within its control and no new date can be agreed within 30 days, all amounts paid, including the Deposit, are refunded.
  • If a stage is not delivered as described in the Proposal, notify us in writing within 15 days of delivery. We will re-perform the deficient work at no cost. If we cannot remedy it within 15 business days, we refund the fees paid for the part of the stage not delivered.

8. Non-refundable items

  • Third-party costs contracted by or for the Client, such as AI model subscriptions, cloud services, software licenses or certification body fees. These are governed by the provider's own terms.
  • Travel, accommodation and other expenses already incurred and approved by the Client.
  • Work performed outside the original scope under an accepted change order, once started.
  • Delays or non-performance caused by the Client, including late access to people, systems or information, or failure to appoint an internal lead or sponsor.
  • Dissatisfaction with business results. Kuxait does not guarantee specific business outcomes, as described in our Terms & Conditions.

9. How to request a refund

  1. Email hello@kuxait.com from the address associated with your account or Proposal, with the subject "Refund request".
  2. Include the invoice number, the service or session concerned, the date, and the reason for the request.
  3. We confirm receipt within 2 business days and respond with a decision within 10 business days. We may ask for additional information.

10. Processing and currency

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days of approval. Depending on your bank or card issuer, funds may take additional time to appear. Refunds are made in the currency of the original payment. Kuxait is not responsible for exchange rate differences or fees charged by your bank, card issuer or payment processor. Where the original payment method is no longer available, we will agree an alternative method with you.

11. Chargebacks

Please contact us before initiating a chargeback. Chargebacks filed without first requesting a refund under this policy, or for services that have been delivered, may be disputed with the supporting documentation of the engagement and may lead to suspension of ongoing services until resolved.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version in force at the date a Proposal is accepted, or a session or product is purchased, applies to that purchase. The current version is always available at kuxait.com/refund-policy.html.
