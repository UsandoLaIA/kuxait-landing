Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Kuxait LLC

Kuxait LLC ("Kuxait", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through our website at kuxait.com (the "Site") and in the course of providing our services, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have.

1. Who we are and scope

Kuxait LLC is a limited liability company organized in the State of Wyoming, United States of America. We provide AI implementation and high-performance team consulting to companies. For personal information collected through the Site, including the contact form and email inquiries, Kuxait acts as the controller (or "business" under U.S. state laws).

When we provide services to a client company, we may process personal information about the client's employees and contractors (for example, during the diagnosis stage of our method). In that case the client is the controller and Kuxait acts as a processor (or "service provider") under the client's instructions. Section 6 describes this in more detail.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Contact and inquiry data: name, work email address, company name, company size and the content of your message when you use our contact form, email us or book a conversation.
  • Business relationship data: billing details, job title, signatures, correspondence, meeting notes and documents exchanged when you become a client or supplier.
  • Session data: attendance records, questions and materials shared during training or leadership sessions.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, language, referring URL, pages visited, time spent and approximate location derived from the IP address. This information is collected through server logs and, if enabled, privacy-respecting analytics as described in section 7.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see section 7.

2.3 Information received during client engagements

  • Information about a client's employees collected through diagnosis forms and interviews: role, responsibilities, where information lives, tools used and self-assessed AI familiarity.
  • Business information contained in the client's systems and documents, which may incidentally include personal information of the client's customers, suppliers or staff.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive categories of personal information (such as health, biometric, financial account or precise geolocation data) and ask that you do not send them to us unless an engagement specifically requires it under a written agreement.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and coordinate a first conversation.
  • To prepare proposals and to deliver, administer and invoice our services.
  • To communicate with you about an engagement, including scheduling, deliverables and support.
  • To operate, secure and improve the Site, including measuring which pages are useful.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including accounting, tax and record-keeping duties.
  • To establish, exercise or defend legal claims and to enforce our terms.
  • With your consent, to send occasional communications about our services. You can withdraw consent at any time.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on individuals.

5. How we share information

We share personal information only as described here:

  • Service providers that process information on our behalf and under contract, such as website hosting, email, form handling, scheduling, video conferencing, document storage, accounting and payment processing. They may use the information only to provide their services to us.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers, accountants and insurers, where necessary.
  • Authorities when required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect our rights, safety or property.
  • Business transfers in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, subject to confidentiality.

We do not share client information with other clients, and we do not publish client names or case details without written consent.

6. AI tools and client data

Our work involves configuring AI tools inside our clients' environments. The following rules apply:

  • AI accounts and workspaces are created in the client's name, under the client's contracts with the provider the client chooses. Kuxait does not act as a reseller of AI providers.
  • Access to client information is granted by role, and Kuxait personnel access only what is necessary for the engagement.
  • Kuxait does not use client information to train or fine-tune models that are available outside the client's environment, and does not disclose it to third parties except the providers the client has contracted.
  • Information about a client's employees collected during the diagnosis stage is used only for that engagement, is reported to the client in aggregated or role-based form where possible, and is returned or deleted at the end of the engagement unless the client instructs otherwise or the law requires retention.
  • A data processing agreement describing these commitments in detail is available to clients on request.

7. Cookies and analytics

The Site uses only the technologies strictly necessary to function, such as remembering your preferences during a visit. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

If we enable analytics, we use a privacy-respecting tool configured without cross-site tracking and, where required by law, only after you give consent through a banner. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some features of the Site. We honor browser "Do Not Track" and Global Privacy Control signals where technically feasible.

9. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, including encryption in transit, access controls based on roles, multi-factor authentication on our systems, and confidentiality obligations for everyone who works with us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; if we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the competent authorities as required by law.

10. International transfers

Kuxait is based in the United States and works with clients in several countries. Personal information may be transferred to and processed in the United States and in the countries where our service providers operate. Where we transfer personal information from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum, or on the recipient's certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where applicable.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your information, subject to legal retention duties.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Receive your information in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In the EU, you can find your authority at edpb.europa.eu; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

To exercise your rights, email hello@kuxait.com. We may need to verify your identity. We respond within 30 days, or within the period required by applicable law. If your information was collected during an engagement for one of our clients, we may redirect your request to that client, who is the controller.

12. California and other U.S. state rights

If you are a resident of California or another U.S. state with a comprehensive privacy law, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to request deletion or correction; to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information or of targeted advertising; and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Kuxait does not sell personal information, does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and does not use sensitive personal information for purposes other than those permitted by law. In the preceding 12 months we collected the categories of information described in section 2 for the purposes described in section 3, and disclosed them only to the service providers described in section 5. You may designate an authorized agent to make requests on your behalf; we will ask for proof of authorization.

13. Children

The Site and our services are intended for businesses and professionals and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Third-party links

The Site may link to third-party websites and services, including AI providers. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, which we encourage you to read.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If changes are material, we will provide additional notice, such as by email to active clients.
