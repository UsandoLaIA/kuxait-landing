The method

Three blocks. Seven stages. An order we don't skip.

Understand first. Then structure the information. Only then apply AI. The same method from 15 people to 1,500.

I · Understand II · Structure & plan III · Apply
01
Diagnosis
Block I — Understand
  1. 01

    Diagnosis

    40 to 90 min per person

    What

    A form and an interview per person: role, where their information lives, tools, AI level.

    Output

    Support needed per person and a readiness map by role.

  2. 02

    Workflow mapping

    Company and per area

    What

    Swimlane maps of how work actually moves, one lane per role. What happens, not what the org chart says.

    Output

    Process maps with handoffs, waits and duplicated work made visible.

  3. 03

    Key findings

    Synthesis

    What

    A fishbone analysis across people, information, technology and process.

    Output

    The core problem in one sentence, and the focus areas for the rest of the work.

  4. 04

    Information architecture

    2 to 4 weeks

    What

    Where each piece of information lives, who owns it and who sees it. One source, no duplicates.

    Output

    Access map per area and the dashboards that were missing.

  5. 05

    Prioritization & roadmap

    2 to 3 days

    What

    Every opportunity on an impact by effort matrix. Quick wins first; the rest planned or discarded in writing.

    Output

    A sequenced roadmap with owners, timing and what each automation needs first.

  6. 06

    AI access & governance

    1 to 2 weeks · parallel to 5 or 7

    What

    Groups, per-role access and accounts on the model you choose, connected to your information. Governance set once.

    Output

    Each person with their own account and only the access their role needs.

  7. 07

    Applied AI & operating rhythm

    Ongoing · biweekly or monthly

    What

    AI applied to the key tasks, in a cycle: apply, measure, retrain. The team learns to maintain its own tools.

    Output

    Measured, accelerated tasks and a team that runs without us.
